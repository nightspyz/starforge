    Honest about the model

    The nuclear binding, the beta-stability valley, the proton-proton reaction and the neutron lifetime are genuinely calculated and reproduce the measured values to within a few percent. The stellar lifetimes, the carbon resonance and the structure-formation limit are scaling laws and schematic thresholds taken from the fine-tuning literature, not full simulations. And varying one constant at a time is itself a debatable move; see What it means.
