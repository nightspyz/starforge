🧬 Protein Synthesis Simulator: from gene to protein

Follow a gene from start to finish. RNA polymerase transcribes the DNA into mRNA. In eukaryotes, the mRNA is capped, the introns are spliced out and a poly(A) tail is added, and then it is exported through a nuclear pore. A ribosome then reads it codon by codon, tRNAs bring the matching amino acids, and the new polypeptide folds into a protein. Click a base in an exon to mutate it and see how the protein changes.

Hover over the DNA or mRNA to inspect it.
Mutate: click a base in an exon to change it (point mutation). Hold Shift and click to delete it (frameshift).
